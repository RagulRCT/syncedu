Investor Grilling: 44 questions from someone who wants to say no

These are written the way a skeptical investor actually talks when they've already decided to pass and are looking for the reason. Each has what they're really thinking, the answer that loses the room, the answer that survives, and the proof you need in your hands before walking in.

Rules for answering a hostile investor
  1. Answer the question in the first sentence. No setup.
  2. If you don't have the number, say "I don't have that yet, here's how I'll get it by [date]". Never bluff.
  3. Concede what's true. "You're right that tutoring is free" is stronger than defending it.
  4. Every strong answer below ends in a number or a test. Fill the [amber] blanks with real facts or remove the claim.

Difficulty: TOUGH BRUTAL DEAL-KILLER. A deal-killer you cannot answer today means you shouldn't pitch yet; use the 90-day test in the Founder Validation first.

What exists today to point at: a working MVP (learner, employer, admin), 66 passing end-to-end checks, signed verifiable credentials, a hiring pipeline with a fee ledger. What does not exist: any real employer, learner, hire or revenue. Do not imply otherwise.
