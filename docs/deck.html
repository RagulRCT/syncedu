The problem

Graduates can't prove they're job-ready. Employers can't tell who is.

For the learner

Spends months and money on courses and certificates. Applies to hundreds of roles. Gets a CV that looks like everyone else's.

For the employer

Screens hundreds of CVs to fill one junior analyst seat. Degrees and course certificates don't predict who can write a JOIN.

The gap

There is no trusted, portable proof of demonstrated skill between "finished a course" and "got hired".

Business model

Free for learners. Employers pay per verified hire.

Who pays

  • Learner: free (acquisition + data)
  • Employer: success fee on a confirmed hire retained 30+ days
  • Later: verification API, institution licences, premium coaching

We avoid charging jobseekers a salary share: restricted or disfavoured in many jurisdictions.
